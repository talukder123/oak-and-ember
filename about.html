OUR STORY

Where Fire Meets Flavor

Since 1971, Oak & Ember has proudly served authentic Canadian cuisine in the heart of Toronto. Our passion for exceptional food begins with locally sourced ingredients, traditional wood-fired cooking, and recipes crafted with care. Every dish is prepared to celebrate Canada's rich culinary heritage while creating memorable dining experiences for every guest who walks through our doors

Our Journey

1971

Oak & Ember Opens

Our first wood-fired restaurant opened in Toronto with a passion for authentic Canadian cuisine.

1988

Expanded Our Menu

Introduced premium steaks, seafood, and handcrafted desserts.

Award Winner

Still Growing

Continuing our tradition of exceptional hospitality and unforgettable flavors.
