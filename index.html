ABOUT US

A Taste of Canada, Crafted with Passion

At Oak & Ember, we bring people together through exceptional food and warm hospitality. Inspired by Canada's rich culinary heritage, every dish is prepared with fresh, locally sourced ingredients and cooked to perfection over an open flame

From juicy wood-fired steaks and handcrafted burgers to fresh seafood and seasonal specialties, our menu celebrates bold flavors and authentic craftsmanship. Whether you're gathering with family, celebrating a special occasion, or enjoying a quiet dinner, Oak & Ember offers a welcoming atmosphere where every meal becomes a memorable experience

Our Promise: Great food, genuine hospitality, and unforgettable moments—crafted over fire, served with heart!
